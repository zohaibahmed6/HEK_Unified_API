# KARO — Business Rules Catalog

**Summary:** 22 business rules were extracted directly from `APIController.cs` and `HSSDA.cs`; the dominant themes are (1) an implicit multi-tenant routing scheme baked into the `encounterId` string, (2) ID encryption/decryption for cross-system identifiers, (3) token-based session validation on every call, and (4) several silently-degraded or stubbed behaviors that look like incomplete features rather than deliberate design.

## Risks
- BR-06 (unauthenticated no-op `SaveScreeningCode`) and BR-09 (hardcoded mock `GetEncounterSummary`) are the two highest-risk items to silently port forward — a migration that faithfully reproduces current behavior would ship broken/fake functionality into the new platform.
- BR-04's hardcoded PHO override for a single named practice (`302_F3H045` → `SCDHB`) is exactly the kind of undocumented, practice-specific special case that is easy to lose during a rewrite and hard to detect until that specific tenant reports a defect.

## Recommendations
- Confirm with the client/business owner whether `GetEncounterSummary` and `SaveScreeningCode` are deprecated, in-progress, or simply broken before deciding whether to port, fix, or drop them in the unified platform.
- Extract all magic numbers/return codes (BR-12, BR-13, BR-16) and hardcoded tenant special-cases (BR-04) into named constants/configuration as part of any rewrite so they survive refactoring visibly.
